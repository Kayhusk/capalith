# Capalith identity assets

The Capalith mark depicts two intact faceted plates joined by one fitted butterfly key: complete skill bundles remain whole while their declared relationship is made visible.

- `capalith-mark.svg` — primary mark for light surfaces.
- `capalith-mark-mono.svg` — one-color mark.
- `capalith-mark-reverse.svg` — reversed one-color mark for dark surfaces.
- `capalith-mark-small.svg` — optical-small mark for 16–32 px use. Both plates use ink so the warm-stone plate does not disappear at favicon size.
- `capalith-mark.png` — 512 px transparent raster export of the primary mark.

Palette:

- ink: `#1A1614`
- signal orange: `#FF6E14`
- warm stone: `#E3E3DB`

Keep the symbol's proportions, central seam, and clearance around the butterfly key intact. Use the optical-small master rather than reducing the primary mark below 32 px.
